## Scientific changes and limits

The F1–F3 normalization correction was already present in the audited
version. This revision extends its evidence to C1/collapse near-empty
cutoffs and explicitly supersedes every frozen radius-cut interpretation,
including the history columns and proposed token wording. F §5.1 contains
the complete map. Unrecomputed C2/C3/turnaround cutoff values are expressly
F3-only historical diagnostics, not corrected F2 outputs.

The recomputed near-empty range is approximately 0.019–0.39 h^-1 Mpc.
Its four cutoffs agree with the independent audit within 0.02%; their
explicit grid-regression budget is 0.2%. The z=3 GM >=1 fraction is about
0.0052. All 84 archived populations retain their original clocks and mass
bins while using the fixed F2 denominator for cumulative volumes.

The 481-node quadrature is retained. Its measured primary tail excess is
0.22–0.24% at >=10 and 0.69–0.75% at >=15. The precision claim is reduced
accordingly; tail tests use independent audit CDF values with respective
0.3% and 0.8% numerical budgets. These do not quantify physical model error.
Signed negative grid residuals remain visible without clipping.

The unchanged sigma approximately 0.99 is a conditional hybrid surrogate.
Overlapping Boolean grains share F's volume-weighted radius shape, while
their intensity and coverage are separately calibrated. Their dimensionless
summed grain-volume density is eta=-ln(1-f_v0), giving 1.33560 / 0.98618,
not F's 0.751969 disjoint filling fraction. The wall mass accounting also
differs. F's spherical EdS-to-wall identification now lists all five
physical assumptions separately from the volume algebra.

G explicitly imports the mean-field assumptions below Leyvraz (2.2):
spatial cluster correlations neglected and morphology neglected or
averaged. Conservative mergers fix the volume history only when the void
expansion rate is prescribed, common and independent of size/age. With
rate-dependent classes, the instantaneous moment can be conserved while
the future weighted expansion changes.

G6 describes a synchronous shell-crossing cohort, not F's independently
reidentified population at successive epochs. In a continued homogeneous
open interior, the zero-transfer coefficient rises from 2/3 toward 1.
The upper bound 1/3 instead applies to the missing source divided by
f_v(1-f_v)/t. The user's audit summary called 2/3 an upper bound; that
description is corrected here. No physical formation, merger or transport
law has been selected.

## Errata and provenance of the independent audit

The two supplied audit files are preserved byte-for-byte; hashes are in the
companion inventory. The audit itself has a mislabeled gel example in
§5.1, G2: **1/t is Stockmayer, not Flory**. Leyvraz §4.5, (4.75)–(4.77)
distinguishes them. Flory's sol fraction q solves q=exp[-t(1-q)] and is
approximately 0.20318787 at t=2, whereas Stockmayer gives 0.5.
The auditor's finite-grid product-kernel value at t=1 has a 4.69% sol
moment deficit. This revision does not certify its cutoff convergence;
the no-omitted-gel condition on G2 remains essential.

FGA-D7 successor clarifications: the frozen F citation for the SvdW04
velocity function should read (A17), with H_s at (A18), rather than (A15).
The E token's original comment describes the monodisperse E2 example;
GE-OD2's reference uses a polydisperse shape, with the same conditional
Boolean/independence/compression assumptions and the extra hybrid
qualifications above. Neither the E value nor its historical record changes.

The external auditor's scratch scripts and raw logs were not supplied.
Their hashes in the audit inventory do not recover their content. They
should be preserved from that session before it expires; this deliverable
does not recreate or impersonate them. Its own tests and logs are distinct.

## Reproducibility and validation

Both evidence producers now write UTF-8 bytes with LF. Raw SHA-256 records
one particular run, including environment and provenance. Cross-platform
numerical comparisons use rtol=1e-11, atol=1e-14 for numeric leaves, exact
keys and scientific labels, excluding environment and raw input hashes.
Equivalent input content must still be verified separately. The comparison
budget covers floating-point differences, not F's quadrature accuracy.

The added shell-crossing test starts from JLH13 (A4)–(A5), varies neighboring
Lagrangian shell labels at fixed time, locates a vanishing radius Jacobian,
and measures the local expansion rate by time differences. It recovers
H_v*t=8/9 without imposing that value as its root equation. Its root xtol
is 5e-14; the two-step finite-difference absolute budget is 2e-7.
